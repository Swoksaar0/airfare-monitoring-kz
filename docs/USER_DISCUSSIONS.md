# User discussions: protocol and evidence template

**Status:** no user discussions have been supplied; 23 October 2026.  
**Related issue:** [#2](https://github.com/Swoksaar0/airfare-monitoring-kz/issues/2)

This is a collection template, not interview findings. The checkpoint calls for 3–5 real discussions with representatives of the target users, or substantive agreement with an actual client.

## Conduct

Invite people who actually plan or have planned domestic flights. Explain the diploma purpose and request permission to take anonymized notes. Do not publish names, contact details, booking references or other identifying data. Record a participant code and relevant travel context.

## Short question guide

1. Describe how you chose a domestic flight on a recent or planned trip.
2. Which information influenced your decision, and what was difficult to obtain or compare?
3. Did you return to the same route and departure date to check its price again? Describe what you did.
4. What would a price-history graph help you decide, if anything?
5. How should an application explain a cached or old quote and missing baggage information?
6. Under what conditions would you want a price alert? What would make alerts annoying or unhelpful?
7. Walk through the prototype scenario, if one is available. Where is the interface unclear?
8. Which proposed capability is unnecessary or less important to you?

Use follow-up questions rather than assuming that the participant wants the proposed features.
